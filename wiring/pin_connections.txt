ESP32-Webradio – Pin-Verbindungen (alle Hardware-Varianten)
================================================================================

Quelle:  src/config/hardware_profile.h
Diagramme: fritzing/circuit_diagram_wroom_tft.*
           fritzing/circuit_diagram_cam_headless.*

Diese Liste entspricht den Hardware-Profilen und den Fritzing-Schaltplänen.
Signalnamen (CS, DCS, DREQ, SCK, MISO, MOSI, SDCS, …) wie in den Fritzing-Parts.


================================================================================
1) Variante wroom_tft
   Profil-ID: wroom_tft
   Board:     ESP32-WROOM (38-Pin)
   Module:    VS1053 + TFT (ST7735) + SPI-SD (über VS1053-Breakout) + Drehencoder
================================================================================

Module:
  - ESP32-WROOM
  - VS1053 Breakout (Adafruit-kompatibel)
  - TFT ST7735
  - Drehencoder KY-040 (CLK/DT) + Taster (SW)
  - SD-Karte über VS1053-Breakout-Slot (SDCS)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  Versorgung / Masse
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESP32 3V3     <->  VS1053  3V3   (beide 3V3-Pins des Breakouts)
ESP32 3V3     <->  VS1053  RST   (RST typisch fest auf 3V3, wie im Fritzing)
ESP32 3V3     <->  TFT     VCC
ESP32 3V3     <->  TFT     RST   (RST typisch fest auf 3V3, wie im Fritzing)
ESP32 GND     <->  VS1053  GND   (beide GND-Pins des Breakouts)
ESP32 GND     <->  TFT     GND
ESP32 GND     <->  Encoder GND
ESP32 GND     <->  Taster  GND


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.2  Gemeinsamer SPI-Bus (ESP32 ↔ VS1053 ↔ TFT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESP32 GPIO18 (SCK)   <->  VS1053 SCK
ESP32 GPIO18 (SCK)   <->  TFT    SCK
ESP32 GPIO19 (MISO)  <->  VS1053 MISO
ESP32 GPIO19 (MISO)  <->  TFT    MISO
ESP32 GPIO23 (MOSI)  <->  VS1053 MOSI
ESP32 GPIO23 (MOSI)  <->  TFT    MOSI


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.3  VS1053 Steuerung
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESP32 GPIO5  <->  VS1053 CS
ESP32 GPIO32 <->  VS1053 DCS
ESP32 GPIO4  <->  VS1053 DREQ


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.4  TFT (nur wroom_tft)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESP32 GPIO15 <->  TFT CS
ESP32 GPIO2  <->  TFT DC
ESP32 GPIO33 <->  TFT BL  (Backlight)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.5  SD (SPI über VS1053-Breakout)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESP32 GPIO21 <->  VS1053 SDCS
  Hinweis: CS der SD-Karte geht an den SDCS-Pin des VS1053-Breakouts.
            SPI-Daten laufen über den gemeinsamen Bus (SCK/MISO/MOSI).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.6  Drehencoder
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESP32 GPIO25 <->  Encoder CLK
ESP32 GPIO26 <->  Encoder DT
ESP32 GPIO27 <->  Taster  SW (Signal)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.7  Sonstiges (Profil, kein Fritzing-Jumper)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESP32 GPIO35  IR-Empfänger (pin_ir=35 im Profil; nicht im Fritzing-Diagramm)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.8  Übersicht GPIO → Signal (wroom_tft)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GPIO   Signal
----   ------
  2    TFT DC
  4    VS1053 DREQ
  5    VS1053 CS
 15    TFT CS
 18    SPI SCK
 19    SPI MISO
 21    SD CS → VS1053 SDCS
 23    SPI MOSI
 25    Encoder CLK
 26    Encoder DT
 27    Encoder SW
 32    VS1053 DCS
 33    TFT BL
 35    IR (optional)


================================================================================
2) Variante cam_headless
   Profil-ID: cam_headless
   Board:     ESP32-CAM AI-Thinker (ohne Kamera betrieben)
   Module:    VS1053 + Drehencoder + onboard SD_MMC
================================================================================

Module:
  - ESP32-CAM (AI-Thinker)
  - VS1053 Breakout
  - Drehencoder KY-040 (CLK/DT) + Taster (SW)
  - SD: onboard SD_MMC (1-Bit), keine externe SPI-SD, kein TFT


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Versorgung / Masse
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESP32-CAM 3V3  <->  VS1053 3V3   (beide 3V3-Pins des Breakouts)
ESP32-CAM 3V3  <->  VS1053 RST   (RST typisch fest auf 3V3, wie im Fritzing)
ESP32-CAM GND  <->  VS1053 GND   (beide GND-Pins des Breakouts)
ESP32-CAM GND  <->  Encoder GND
ESP32-CAM GND  <->  Taster  GND


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.2  Gemeinsamer SPI-Bus (ESP32-CAM ↔ VS1053)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESP32-CAM GPIO18 (SCK)   <->  VS1053 SCK
ESP32-CAM GPIO19 (MISO)  <->  VS1053 MISO
ESP32-CAM GPIO23 (MOSI)  <->  VS1053 MOSI

  Kein TFT an diesem SPI-Bus.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.3  VS1053 Steuerung
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESP32-CAM GPIO5  <->  VS1053 CS
ESP32-CAM GPIO32 <->  VS1053 DCS
ESP32-CAM GPIO4  <->  VS1053 DREQ


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.4  TFT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Nicht vorhanden (kein Display in cam_headless).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.5  SD (onboard SD_MMC, 1-Bit)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Interne Board-Verbindungen, keine externen Jumper zum VS1053:

ESP32-CAM GPIO14  =  SD_MMC CLK   (onboard)
ESP32-CAM GPIO15  =  SD_MMC CMD   (onboard)
ESP32-CAM GPIO2   =  SD_MMC D0    (onboard)

  pin_sd_cs = -1 (kein externes SPI-SD-CS)
  VS1053 SDCS wird in dieser Variante nicht verdrahtet.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.6  Drehencoder
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ESP32-CAM GPIO25 <->  Encoder CLK
ESP32-CAM GPIO26 <->  Encoder DT
ESP32-CAM GPIO27 <->  Taster  SW (Signal)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.7  Hinweise CAM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Ohne Kamera betreiben: GPIO 5/18/19/23/25/26/27/32 sind sonst Kamerapads.
  - GPIO 12/13/16/17 für Extrafunktionen meiden (CAM/Flash-Strapping).
  - Kein IR (pin_ir = -1).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.8  Übersicht GPIO → Signal (cam_headless)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GPIO   Signal
----   ------
  2    SD_MMC D0 (onboard)
  4    VS1053 DREQ
  5    VS1053 CS
 14    SD_MMC CLK (onboard)
 15    SD_MMC CMD (onboard)
 18    SPI SCK
 19    SPI MISO
 23    SPI MOSI
 25    Encoder CLK
 26    Encoder DT
 27    Encoder SW
 32    VS1053 DCS
